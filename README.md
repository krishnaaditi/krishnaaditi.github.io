### 🌷 A Little About Me

I am a **Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Wayne State University**, Detroit, Michigan, USA, and a researcher at the **SOL Lab — Scalable Optimization and Learning**.

I feel **blessed to be the first Ph.D. student of Professor Prashant Khanduri**, my doctoral advisor, and grateful for the opportunity to learn and grow under his guidance. 🌱

My research focuses on **Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)**, particularly on understanding **why, where, and how hallucinations emerge inside these models**.

I investigate how **visual and textual representations evolve across model layers** and how these internal signals can be leveraged for **hallucination detection and mitigation**, with the broader goal of developing **grounded, reliable, interpretable, and trustworthy multimodal models**. ✨
