Ph.D. Student, Wayne State University Detroit, Michigan aditi1@wayne.edu
Research focus. I work on multimodal large language models (MLLMs), with an emphasis on hallucination detection and mitigation, spurious-correlation removal, representation geometry, and trustworthy multimodal reasoning.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Wayne State University. My research focuses on understanding what happens inside vision-language models when they make unreliable predictions, and on designing methods that improve grounding, robustness, and reasoning.

I am especially interested in sample-specific internal representations, hallucination-critical layers, intrinsic dimension, multimodal alignment, and machine unlearning for reliable AI.
